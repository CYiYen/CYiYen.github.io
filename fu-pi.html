04 — 2024 — Sensor-driven performance · National Taichung Theater LAB X

Fu Pi負皮

一場以身體感測為主題的舞蹈演出，我負責舞台影像。A dance performance built around body sensing; I made the stage video.

Medium
Live performance with body sensing: depth camera, thermal and skeleton tracking, real-time projection, VR
Project
Team of six; shown here is the stage imagery I made
Role
Stage imagery: the full cue sequence (cue 0–7) for the dual-screen wall, 3840 × 1080, built in TouchDesigner
Shown
2024 National Taichung Theater LAB X — “Young Artists’ Studio: Body Sensing” (selected), work period Jun–Nov 2024, showing 23 Nov 2024
影像 · 左右滑動9 張
01

《負皮》入選台中國家歌劇院 LAB X 計畫，取材自《聊齋》的〈畫皮〉。作品把皮膚想成肉身與網路身分之間的一層，想問身體被感測器讀過之後，還剩下什麼。舞者在半透明布幕後移動，攝影機讀取身體的輪廓、溫度與骨架，讀到的資料再化成光，投回舞台上。

Fu Pi was selected for the National Taichung Theater's LAB X programme. It takes its cue from the Liaozhai tale Painted Skin, treats skin as a layer between the flesh and an online identity, and asks what is left of a body once sensors have read it. A dancer moves behind a translucent curtain while cameras read the outline, heat and skeleton of the body; the readings return to the stage as projected light.

02

我負責舞台影像，也就是演出中雙螢幕牆上的全部影像段落（cue 0–7），用 TouchDesigner 與 Blender 製作，配合舞者的段落切換。

My part was the stage video: every cue (0–7) on the dual-screen wall during the performance, made in TouchDesigner and Blender and timed to the dancer's sections.

03

計畫從 2024 年 6 月進行到 11 月，11 月 23 日在歌劇院公開呈現。

The project ran from June to November 2024, with a public showing at the theater on 23 November.

My part
The stage imagery — every video cue in the show
Tools
TouchDesigner, Blender
Format
Dual screen, 3840 × 1080
Team
Six members; sensing, dramaturgy and performance by the others
