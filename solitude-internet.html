03 — 2024 — Kinetic optical installation

Solitude Internet

依每日新聞情緒分數旋轉的紅色濾光片；唯有視線、孔洞與濾光片對齊時，被遮蔽的影像才會顯現。A red filter rotated by the day's news sentiment; the concealed image appears only when eye, aperture and filter align.

01

程式每日擷取台灣熱門新聞標題並進行情緒分析，將結果換算為單一分數，以此控制伺服馬達的角度，轉動盒內的紅色濾光片。社會整體的情緒因此成為一片緩慢轉動的濾鏡。

A script collects Taiwan's trending news headlines each day, analyses their sentiment and reduces the result to a single score. The score sets the angle of a servo motor, which rotates a red filter inside a small box. The collective mood of the day becomes a slowly turning filter.

02

兩面相對的投影幕疊合兩層影像：上層為代表資訊過量的綠色雜訊，下層為紅色的裸身影像。肉眼僅能看見雜訊；觀眾需移動身體、將視線對準盒上的孔洞，且濾光片恰好轉至孔前，紅色影像才會顯現。

Two facing projection screens layer two images: green noise representing information overload, and beneath it a red image of a bare body. To the naked eye only the noise is visible. The red image appears only when the viewer aligns their eye with the aperture in the box at the moment the filter passes in front of it.

03

演算法的過濾通常在程式中瞬間完成；本作將其放慢為可被觀察、也可能錯過的光學過程。林茲展出版本為木製箱體，卡托維茲版本改以透明壓克力重製，使內部機構可見。作品論述另於 2024 年科技藝術國際學術研討會（ISAT）口頭發表。

Algorithmic filtering normally happens instantaneously in software; this work slows it into an optical process that can be observed, or missed. The Linz version used a wooden box; for Katowice it was rebuilt in clear acrylic to expose the mechanism. The accompanying paper was presented at ISAT 2024.
