03 — 2024 — Kinetic optical installation

Solitude Internet

新聞的情緒分數轉動一片紅色濾光片，眼睛對準孔洞，才看得到底下的影像。A red filter turned by the day's news sentiment; the hidden image shows only when eye, hole and filter line up.

Medium
Box with rotating red acrylic filter, servo, Arduino, two facing projection screens, live news sentiment feed
Versions
v1.0 wooden box 45 × 45 × 10 cm (Linz) · v2.0 acrylic box 27 × 27 × 7.5 cm (Katowice)
Made with
One co-creator; shown here is the work as exhibited
Shown
2024 Ars Electronica Festival Campus Exhibition, POSTCITY Linz — Anthropogenic Encroachment · Nov 2024 Rondo Sztuki, Katowice — European City of Science
01

程式每天抓台灣的熱門新聞標題做情緒分析，算出當天的情緒分數，再用這個分數控制一顆伺服馬達，轉動小盒子裡的紅色濾光片。整個社會的情緒，就這樣變成一片慢慢轉動的濾鏡。

Each day a script collects Taiwan's trending news headlines, runs sentiment analysis on them and reduces the result to one score. The score sets the angle of a servo, which turns a red filter inside a small box. The day's collective mood becomes a filter, turning slowly.

02

兩面投影幕面對面，疊著兩層影像。上層是綠色雜訊，代表太多的資訊；下層是一個紅色的裸身。肉眼只看得到雜訊。觀眾得移動身體，把眼睛湊到盒子的孔上，濾光片又剛好轉到孔前，底下的紅色影像才會浮出來。

Two facing screens show two layers: green noise standing for too much information, and beneath it a red image of a bare body. To the naked eye there is only noise. A visitor has to move, bring an eye to the hole in the box and catch the moment the filter covers it; only then does the red image appear.

03

演算法的過濾通常一瞬間就在程式裡做完。這件作品把它放慢，變成一段看得見、也可能對不上的光學過程。林茲展出的是木盒版；到卡托維茲時改用透明壓克力重做，讓裡面的機構也看得到。作品的論述另外在 2024 年科技藝術國際學術研討會（ISAT）口頭發表。

Filtering that software does in an instant is slowed down into an optical process you can watch, and miss. The Linz version was a wooden box; for Katowice it was rebuilt in clear acrylic so the mechanism shows. The ideas behind the piece were presented as a paper at ISAT 2024.

Concept
Algorithmic filtering as a material boundary
Build
Arduino Leonardo, MG996R servo, acrylic filter, two-layer projection
Data
Google Trends scraper, translation, VADER sentiment (−1 to +1)
Paper
ISAT 2024, Taipei National University of the Arts
