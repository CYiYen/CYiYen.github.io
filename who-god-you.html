11 — 2021 — Machine-learning game · research demo

WHO GOD YOU基於機器學習之體感骨架辨識遊戲

用 Unity 與姿勢辨識做的找人遊戲。A pose-matching game built with Unity and a pose model.

Medium
Unity, Google Teachable Machine (pose model), webcam
Team
Two people
Shown
TAICHI 2021, 7th Taiwan CHI conference, Taipei — demo paper
01

規則像「威利在哪裡」，但要找的不是穿著，而是姿勢。街上有二十個低多邊形路人，其中一個擺著不同的姿勢；玩家要在攝影機前做出同一個姿勢，才能找到他。

A Where's-Wally game where the target is picked out not by clothing but by posture. Twenty low-poly passers-by wander a street and one of them holds a different pose; the player finds them by striking that pose in front of a webcam.

02

姿勢模型用 Teachable Machine 訓練，分成四類（閒置，加上三關各一），即時接進 Unity，不需要手把。三關分別是後仰、前彎、半蹲，玩起來也像在做伸展。

A pose model trained in Teachable Machine (four classes: neutral plus one per level) streams into Unity, so no controller is needed. The three levels use back-bend, forward-bend and half-squat, so playing is also a little stretching.

Engine
