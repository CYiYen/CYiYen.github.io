Soundscape City音景城市

十段日常錄音，堆成一座城市的影像。Ten everyday recordings, built up into the image of a city.

Medium
Ten field recordings, Reaper, TouchDesigner, speech recognition, StreamDiffusion
Format
Single-channel generative video, short study
Role
Concept, sound editing, TouchDesigner system, AI pipeline (solo)
Year
2024

這段影片有聲音，點播放列的喇叭可以開啟。

01

十段日常錄音，包括新竹六樓的風、高雄的燈塔、南寮漁港的海浪，先在 Reaper 裡剪出節奏點。每個節奏點在格狀地圖上升起一個方塊，聲音就這樣在 TouchDesigner 裡堆成一座城市。

Ten recordings from daily life, among them wind on a sixth floor in Hsinchu, a Kaohsiung lighthouse and the sea at Nanliao harbour, are cut in Reaper and their percussive hits extracted. Each hit raises a block on a grid, and the sounds pile up into a city in TouchDesigner.

02

系統也設計成能接收說話的關鍵字。語音辨識抓出詞語後，StreamDiffusion 依照這些詞重新生成城市的畫面，不同地點、不同時間的錄音因此疊在同一個畫面裡。

The system can also take spoken keywords: speech recognition picks out words and StreamDiffusion repaints the city from them, so recordings from different places and times end up in one picture.

Sound

Visual
