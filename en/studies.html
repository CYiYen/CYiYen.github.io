S1 — 2026 — Fulldome geometry & particle systems · 235° fisheye

Fulldome 2024–2026

Fulldome content for a 2024 campus presentation, and subsequent generative systems for the dome.

Engine
TouchDesigner, GLSL POP generators, cube-map → fisheye
Format
1280 × 1280 fisheye, 235°, 60 fps · 4096 × 4096 masters
Screened
Future Vision Lab 2024, C-LAB Dome Theatre, 22 Dec 2024 — NTHU × Shih Chien presentation: programme compiled by the author; two pieces and the opening transition
Status
In development, Sept 2026
01

For the NTHU × Shih Chien University student presentation at Future Vision Lab 2024 (C-LAB Dome Theatre, 22 December 2024), a team production, compiled all participants' videos into the full programme and produced two fulldome pieces and the opening transition: a skin-like sphere that swells open, and a black hole that draws particles inward. Both pieces were projected across the full dome above the seated audience. Work in the fulldome format has continued since.

02

Two systems for fulldome projection followed. The first is a 70-second loop morphing between seven procedural geometries (rings, lattice, tunnel, wave, caustic, depth, spiral) every ten seconds. Because the dome exceeds a standard 180° lens, the image is rendered via a cube map and converted to a 235° fisheye.

03

The second is a particle study on emotional self-depletion. Particles contract towards a black centre, accelerating as they approach, then rebound outward, each cycle shorter than the last. Layered speeds, depth-dependent particle size and a dark centre give the motion a sense of inward pull.
