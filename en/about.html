Scene Properties — About

Chen Yi-Yen陳羿言

Interactive installation · Real-time visuals · Sound · Hardware & software integration

My MFA research comprised three works examining the physical conditions required for a message to be transmitted. The final work, Beacon Silence, transmits seven channels of sound through custom Li-Fi circuits and was exhibited at VA Hub, Taipei, in July 2026. Production work includes stage video for a theatre performance; the compiled programme, two fulldome pieces and the opening transition for a campus presentation at the Future Vision Lab showcase; image generation and installation for Solar in LuxuryLogico's solo exhibition at MoCA Taipei; and partial development and testing of the directional-speaker tracking system for Echo Chamber at the C-LAB Sound Art Festival. Primary tools: TouchDesigner, Blender and Premiere Pro.
