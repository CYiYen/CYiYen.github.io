01 — 2026 — Interactive sound installation · Li-Fi

Beacon Silence

Seven laser beams each transmit one part of a composition; when a beam is blocked, its part falls silent.

This video has sound; turn it on from the controls.

01

Seven green laser beams cross a darkened corridor. Each beam carries one part of the same composition to a solar panel on the opposite wall, where it is converted back into sound. The piece is complete only when all seven beams arrive.

02

The work requires no deliberate interaction. On entering the corridor, a visitor's body interrupts one or more beams, and the corresponding parts drop out. There is therefore no ideal listening position; what each visitor hears depends on where they stand.

03

The more people present, the quieter the corridor becomes. When all seven beams are blocked, the space is full and the sound is gone. The work defines this state as fading rather than failure: the system continues to run, and what remains audible is silence.

04

The system consists of seven independent Li-Fi links. A DFPlayer Mini plays the audio, and a TIP122 stage modulates the brightness of a 12 V green laser; a 0.63 W solar panel on the opposite side detects the variation in light and converts it back into audio for an active speaker. Several transmitters are mounted on motorised rails and move slowly, so the beam positions shift continuously.
