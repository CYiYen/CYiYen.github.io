Luminous Membrane

A 3D-printed sculpture illuminated from within by LED lines.

Medium
Blender sculpt, 3D-printed modules, translucent membrane, LEDs, Arduino
Format
Wall of repeated modules with one illuminated organic core (renders; prototype modules printed)
Role
Concept, sculpting, print files, electronics (solo)
Year
2023
01

The work continues an observation of biological interaction, starting from the encounter between a person and a mosquito within personal space. The anthropologist Edward T. Hall divided the space around a person into four zones: public, social, personal and intimate. Personal space is controlled by the individual, and others need permission to enter. A mosquito, driven by the instinct to survive and reproduce, crosses into it; a person answers with a slap to restore order.

02

Luminous Membrane turns this invaded private zone into a glowing layer of flesh: in a wall of identical red modules, one holds a soft pink core lit from within, and lines of light extend outward from it to suggest infection and spread after the intrusion.

03

The core was sculpted in Blender, prepared for 3D printing and lit with Arduino-controlled LED lines. The wall images are renders; the photograph shows the printed piece.
