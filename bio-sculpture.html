Luminous Membrane

3D 列印的造形，用 LED 從內部打光。A 3D-printed form lit from inside by LED lines.

Medium
Blender sculpt, 3D-printed modules, translucent membrane, LEDs, Arduino
Format
Wall of repeated modules with one illuminated organic core (renders; prototype modules printed)
Role
Concept, sculpting, print files, electronics (solo)
Year
2023
01

一整面相同的紅色模組，其中一格放著從內部發光的粉紅色軟核。作品想的是身體的邊界在哪裡，列印的外殼可以一再複製，發光的膜不行。

A wall of identical red modules, one of which holds a soft pink core lit from inside. The piece asks where a body ends: the printed shell can be repeated, the lit membrane cannot.

02

核心先在 Blender 雕塑，轉成 3D 列印檔，再用 Arduino 控制 LED 燈條從內部打光。整面牆的圖是渲染，照片是實際列印出來的作品。

The core was sculpted in Blender, prepared for 3D printing and lit with LED lines driven by an Arduino. The wall images are renders; the photo shows the printed piece.

3D
Blender sculpting, STL preparation
Fabrication
3D-printed modules
Electronics
Arduino, LED lines
Period
Oct–Dec 2023
