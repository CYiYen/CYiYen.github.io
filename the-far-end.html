08 — 2022 — Experimental theatre · media × dance

The Infinite Depth

無盡深邃的那端

結合鏡面裝置與即時投影的舞蹈演出。A dance performance combining a mirror installation with live projection.

01

鏡面裝置承接即時投影，並將其碎裂反射。舞者與自身殘影互動、模仿，最終被殘影取代；液晶螢幕同時呈現另一條敘事線。

A mirror installation receives the live projection and reflects it in fragments. The dancer interacts with and imitates their own afterimage before being replaced by it, while LCD screens carry a parallel narrative.

02

故事描述一位身陷謠言的人：社群媒體上的片段資訊如何拼湊出與本人不符的形象，以及當事人在此壓力下的自我懷疑。

The narrative follows a person caught in rumour: how fragments of information on social media assemble an image that does not match them, and the self-doubt that follows.
