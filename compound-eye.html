06 — 2023 — VR environment · Blender → Unity

Compound Eye of Technology科技複眼

以網路個人資料為題的雙場景 VR 作品。A two-scene VR work on personal data online.

01

作品將每個網路帳號視為一隻眼睛，既收集觀點，也將觀點傳播出去。VR 中包含兩個空間：以個人與信仰符號構成的圓形大廳，以及以柱體代表被儲存之個人資料的鏡面房間。

The work treats each online account as an eye that both gathers and disseminates points of view. It comprises two spaces: a circular hall built from symbols of personal and religious attachment, and a mirrored room whose pillars represent stored personal data.

02

體驗初期觀者可自由移動並選擇觀看方向；隨場景轉換，觀者位置被固定，視角成為房間中的其中一個畫面。大廳中戴面具的人物以 Blender 建模。

Initially the viewer moves freely and chooses where to look; as the scene changes, their position becomes fixed and their view becomes one of the screens within the room. The masked figures in the hall were modelled in Blender.
