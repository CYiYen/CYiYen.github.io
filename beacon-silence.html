01 — 2026 — Interactive sound installation · Li-Fi

Beacon Silence

七道雷射各傳一個聲部，人擋住哪道光，哪個聲部就安靜。Seven laser beams each carry one part of the music. Block a beam and that part goes quiet.

Medium
Li-Fi (visible light communication), laser, solar cell, custom PCB, Arduino
Setup
7 laser beams · 7 audio channels · 6.5 × 2 m corridor
Sound
7-part abstract composition, 3′22″
Role
Concept, electronics, composition, spatial design (solo work)
Shown
Unseen Circuits, VA Hub, Taipei, 1–3 July 2026 · MFA thesis work, NTHU

這段影片有聲音，點播放列的喇叭可以開啟。

01

暗走道裡橫著七道綠色雷射。每道光各載著同一首曲子的一個聲部，打到對面牆上的太陽能板，再還原成聲音。七道光都到了，曲子才是完整的。

Seven green laser beams cross a dark corridor. Each carries one part of the same piece of music to a solar panel on the far wall, which turns the light back into sound. Only when all seven arrive is the piece complete.

02

觀眾不用特別做什麼。人一走進來，身體就會擋住某道光，那個聲部跟著消失。所以現場沒有標準的聆聽位置，每個人聽到的，都是自己身體擋出來的版本。

Visitors do not need to do anything. A body walking in blocks one beam or another, and that part drops out. There is no ideal listening spot; what each person hears depends on where their body is.

03

人越多，走道越安靜。七道光全被擋住的時候，房間裡站滿了人，聲音卻全沒了。我把這一刻叫做「消隱」，不是故障。系統還在運作，這時聽得到的只剩寂靜。

The more people there are, the quieter the corridor gets. With all seven beams blocked, the room is full and the music is gone. I call this moment fading rather than failure: the system is still running, and what is left to hear is silence.

04

技術上是七組各自獨立的 Li-Fi 傳輸。DFPlayer Mini 播放音訊，經過 TIP122 控制 12 V 綠色雷射的亮度；對面牆上 0.63 W 的太陽能板接收光的明暗變化，還原成音訊送進主動式喇叭。其中幾組發射端裝在馬達滑軌上緩慢移動，所以光束的位置一直在變。

Technically these are seven separate Li-Fi links. A DFPlayer Mini plays the audio and a TIP122 stage uses it to vary the brightness of a 12 V green laser; a 0.63 W solar panel on the opposite wall picks up the changes in light and feeds an active speaker. Some of the transmitters sit on motorised rails and move slowly, so the beam positions keep changing.
