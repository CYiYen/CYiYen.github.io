02 — 2025 — Tactile sound / VR installation

Paper Shell Echo紙殼迴聲

沒有喇叭，聲音靠紙殼本身的振動傳出來；頭盔裡播放穹頂影像。No loudspeaker: the sound comes from the cardboard shell itself, with dome imagery in the headset.

Medium
Cardboard enclosure, Taptic Engine linear actuators, Bluetooth audio, VR headset, 360° video
Format
Single-visitor, lying down, ~4 min
Role
Concept, structure, electronics, 360° content
Shown
CGW 2025 (Computer Graphics Workshop), Taipei — Honorable Mention
01

穹頂展演用聲音包圍整個身體，但聲音從喇叭、穹幕到座位的這段路，觀眾通常不會注意到。《紙殼迴聲》把穹頂縮小成一個紙板殼，讓人躺下來，把頭放進去。

Dome shows surround the body with sound, but the path that sound takes, from speaker to dome surface to seat, usually goes unnoticed. Paper Shell Echo shrinks the dome into a cardboard shell you lie down and rest your head in.

02

裝置裡沒有喇叭。殼內嵌著線性共振致動器，把音訊轉成振動，紙板本身就是發聲的地方。聲音一部分從空氣傳到耳朵，一部分從貼著紙面的後腦傳進來。

There is no loudspeaker. Linear resonant actuators set into the shell turn the audio into vibration, and the cardboard itself becomes the sounding surface. Part of the sound reaches the ears through the air; part comes through the back of the head where it rests against the shell.

03

VR 頭盔裡播放的 360 度內容，是我在 2024 年未來視覺實驗室成果展做的穹頂開場與結尾，放進我為這件作品寫的虛擬穹頂裡重播。

The VR headset plays 360° content: my fulldome opening and ending from the 2024 Future Vision Lab showcase, replayed inside a virtual dome I wrote for this piece.

Concept
Sound conduction as a material interface
Build
Cardboard structure, Taptic Engine LRA array, Bluetooth audio receiver
Content
My FVL 2024 fulldome opening/ending, replayed in a custom virtual dome simulator
Recognition
CGW 2025 Honorable Mention (artwork track)
