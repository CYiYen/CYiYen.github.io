10 — 2020 — Outdoor light installation

Quantum Transmission Phone Booth千里傳訊直通彼心 量子傳輸電話亭

會隨說話聲音發光的電話亭。A phone booth whose lights respond to the caller's voice.

Medium
Acrylic, wood, LED strips, telephone, Arduino · 100 × 100 × 240 cm
With
Shih Chien Architecture × TNUA New Media × NTU Civil Engineering
Role
Arduino lighting + telephone control, structure fabrication
Shown
2020 C-LAB Lightweight cross-school workshop, lawn in front of Building B
01

電話亭是一個人的小房間。對著這座電話亭的話筒說話，亭內的燈和頂上的燈管會跟著亮暗，草坪上的人看得到有人在說話，卻聽不到內容。

A phone booth is a room for one. Here, speaking into the handset drives the light inside the booth and the tubes above it, so people on the lawn can see that someone is talking without hearing what is said.

02

這是在 C-LAB 和建築系、土木系學生一起進行的一週工作營作品。我負責用 Arduino 讀取話筒的聲音、控制 LED，也參與亭體的製作。

Made in a one-week workshop at C-LAB with architecture and civil engineering students. My part was the Arduino that reads the handset audio and drives the LEDs, and building the booth body.

Electronics
Arduino audio-to-light control, LED strips
Fabrication
Booth body in wood and acrylic
Team
Cross-school, 3 departments
Venue
C-LAB, Taipei
