Studies — 2026

即時影像研究Real-time studies 2026

兩個還在進行的 TouchDesigner 研究，一個是跟著姿勢生長的植物，一個是穹頂用的生成影像。Two TouchDesigner studies in progress: a plant that responds to posture, and generative loops for a dome.

S1 — 2026 — Pose-driven generative plant · TouchDesigner POP

Botanic姿勢驅動的植物

會隨觀者姿勢改變的生成植物。A generated plant that changes with the viewer's posture.

Engine
TouchDesigner 2025, POP particles, L-system
Input
Webcam silhouette, numpy pose analysis
Output
1280 × 1280 artwork + monitor view
Status
Working prototype, Sept 2026
01

攝影機看著一個人。彎腰時，幼苗長得低、往下垂；站直時，它往上長；張開手臂，側枝就往兩邊展開、開花。植物是一套 L-system，由六種幾何（主幹、枝、節、葉、花、花瓣）組成，靠三個身體讀數驅動，分別是身體多直、手臂多開、蹲得多低。

A camera watches a person. Bend forward and the seedling stays low and droops; stand tall and it shoots up; open your arms and the branches spread wide and bloom. The plant is an L-system of six independent geometry classes (trunk, branches, nodes, leaves, flowers, petals) driven by three body readings: upright, arms, crouch.

02

輪廓先降成 32 × 24 的網格，再用身體自己的範圍去量，所以站離攝影機遠或近都不影響。生長階段、分枝角度、重力與節間長度都寫成這些讀數的運算式；周圍的霧是從骨架重新取樣的粒子。

The silhouette is reduced to a 32 × 24 grid and measured against the body's own bounds, so distance from the camera does not matter. Growth phase, branch angle, gravity and internode length are all written as expressions of those readings; a particle cloud resampled from the skeleton adds the haze.

S2 — 2026 — Fulldome geometry & particle systems · 235° fisheye

Fulldome 2024–2026穹頂影像

2024 年穹頂成果展的開場與結尾，以及之後的循環影像。Opening and ending for a 2024 fulldome showcase, and later loops.

Engine
TouchDesigner, GLSL POP generators, cube-map → fisheye
Format
1280 × 1280 fisheye, 235°, 60 fps · 4096 × 4096 masters
Screened
FVL dome showcase, C-LAB, Dec 2024 — programme opening & ending
Status
In development, Sept 2026
01

2024 年 12 月未來視覺實驗室的穹頂成果展，我做了整場的開場與結尾。開場是一顆像皮膚一樣膨開的球體，結尾是一個黑洞，把整晚的粒子收回去。兩段都投在觀眾頭頂的整面穹幕上。之後我一直持續在做穹頂影像。

For the Future Vision Lab dome showcase in December 2024 I made the programme's opening and ending: a skin-like sphere that swells open, and a black hole that gathers the evening's particles back in at the close. Both played across the whole dome above the seated audience. I have kept working in the dome format since.

02

之後做了兩套穹頂用的系統。第一套是 70 秒的循環，每 10 秒在七種程序幾何之間變形（環、格、隧道、波、焦散、深度、螺旋）。穹頂的視角比一般 180 度魚眼更寬，所以畫面先渲染成 cube map，再轉成 235 度魚眼。

Since then I have built two systems for fulldome projection. The first is a 70-second loop that morphs between seven procedural geometries (rings, lattice, tunnel, wave, caustic, depth, spiral) every ten seconds. Because the dome is wider than a standard 180° lens, it is rendered through a cube map to a 235° fisheye.

03

第二套是關於情緒自我消耗的粒子練習。粒子往黑色的中心收縮，越接近越快，再向外彈開，每一輪都比上一輪短。速度分層、粒子大小對應深度、中心保持全黑，讓動態看起來像是被往內吸。

The second is a particle study about emotional self-consumption. Particles contract toward a black centre, faster as they get closer, then spring back out, each cycle shorter than the last. Layered speeds, size tied to depth and a centre kept dark make the motion read as a pull inward.
