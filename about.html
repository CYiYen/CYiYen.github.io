陳羿言Chen Yi-Yen

我做互動裝置與即時影像，電路、程式到現場架設，多半自己動手。I make interactive installations and real-time visuals, often building the circuits, the software and the installation myself.

我做互動裝置與即時影像。碩士期間的三件作品圍繞同一個問題，一則訊息要送達，中間得經過哪些物理條件。最後一件《Beacon Silence》用我自己做的 Li-Fi 電路傳送七聲道聲音，2026 年 7 月在台北 VA Hub 展出。個人創作之外，我也參與團隊和其他創作者的製作，包括劇場演出的舞台影像、穹頂成果展的開場與結尾、豪華朗機工在台北當代藝術館個展中《日光域》的影像生成與布展，以及 C-LAB 一件聲音裝置的系統測試。平常最常用 TouchDesigner、Unity、Blender 和 Arduino。

I make interactive installations and real-time visuals. My three MFA works asked one question: what physically has to happen for a message to arrive. The last of them, Beacon Silence, carried seven channels of sound over Li-Fi circuits I built, and was shown at VA Hub, Taipei, in July 2026. I have also worked on other people's productions: stage video for a theatre piece, the opening and ending of a fulldome showcase, image content and installation for one work in LuxuryLogico's exhibition at MoCA Taipei, and system testing for a sound installation at C-LAB. I mostly work in TouchDesigner, Unity, Blender and Arduino.
