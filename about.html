陳羿言Chen Yi-Yen

互動裝置・即時影像・聲音・實體運算Interactive installation · Real-time visuals · Sound · Physical computing

碩士階段以三件作品探討訊息傳遞所需的物理條件。其中《Beacon Silence》以自製 Li-Fi 電路傳輸七聲道聲音，2026 年 7 月於台北 VA Hub 展出。另參與團隊作品及其他創作者作品的製作，包括劇場演出舞台影像、穹頂成果展開場與結尾影像、豪華朗機工台北當代藝術館個展中《日光域》的影像生成與布展，以及 C-LAB 聲音裝置的系統測試。常用工具為 TouchDesigner、Unity、Blender 與 Arduino。

My MFA research comprised three works examining the physical conditions required for a message to be transmitted. The final work, Beacon Silence, transmits seven channels of sound through custom Li-Fi circuits and was exhibited at VA Hub, Taipei, in July 2026. Production work includes stage video for a theatre performance, the opening and closing sequences of a fulldome showcase, image generation and installation for Solar in LuxuryLogico's solo exhibition at MoCA Taipei, and system testing for a sound installation at C-LAB. Primary tools: TouchDesigner, Unity, Blender and Arduino.
