08 — 2022 — Video interactive installation

Naked House

感官全裸

每個房間代表一個感官的模型屋，以感測器觸發影像。A model house where each room stands for a sense, with sensor-triggered video.

Medium
Blender, Max/MSP, Arduino, cast objects, Ableton Live · 230 × 190 × 160 cm
Team
Four members
Role
Max + Arduino integration, Blender character design
Shown
2022 Endless Migration, BFA graduation exhibition, Huashan 1914 Creative Park East 2D, Taipei
01

頭部是感官集中的地方，眼、耳、鼻、口連在同一套情緒裡。《感官全裸》把「家」的概念延伸到五官，讓每個器官在模型屋裡各有一個房間。

The head is where the senses gather; eyes, ears, nose and mouth feed into one emotional system. Naked House stretches the idea of home to the face and gives each organ its own room in a model house.

02

屋子上的感測器會觸發 Blender 做的室內動畫，靠近窗戶看，裡面播放的內容就會改變。

Sensors on the house trigger interior animations made in Blender, so looking closer at a window changes what plays inside.

Integration
Max/MSP ↔ Arduino sensor logic and video switching
3D
Character design and animation in Blender
Team
4 members, BFA New Media Art, TNUA
Venue
Huashan 1914 Creative Park, Taipei
